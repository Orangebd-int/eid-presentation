1. Introduction

As a PSC operator, you work at the Public Service Center counter or back office to help citizens obtain their national electronic identity (eID). You use the PSC Operator Portal in your web browser to:

  • Review online eID applications submitted by citizens from home
  • Approve, reject, escalate, or request more documents from applicants
  • Enrol citizens in person at the counter (assisted enrolment)
  • Resolve fraud and identity-mismatch cases as a supervisor
  • Monitor system activity through audit logs
  • Retry registry updates when the Civil Registry connection is offline
Your responsibility: Every decision you make is recorded in the audit trail. Always select the correct SOP reason code and add clear verification notes when approving with mismatches or manual overrides.

Tip: Set up an authenticator app (TOTP) under Security & MFA as soon as possible. This protects your account and is required for secure daily operations.

2. Sign in & sign out

How to sign in

  1. Open the eID website and click Sign in (or go directly to the login page).
  2. Enter your work email address or eID number (for example, your staff eID starting with ID-SXM-…).
  3. Enter your password and click continue.
  4. Complete the second verification step:
    • SMS: Enter the 6-digit code sent to your registered mobile phone.
    • Authenticator app: Enter the 6-digit code shown in your app (Google Authenticator, Microsoft Authenticator, etc.).
  5. After successful login, you are taken automatically to the PSC Operator Portal dashboard.
Staff login rule: You must sign in with your eID number or email address. Signing in with your username alone (for example, psc_admin) is not accepted for staff accounts.

How to sign out

  1. Look at the top-right corner of any PSC page.
  2. Click Sign Out.
  3. Always sign out when leaving your workstation unattended.

Returning to the public citizen website

At the bottom of the left menu, click Exit to Public Portal to leave the PSC portal and go to the public citizen homepage. This does not sign you out.

5. Enrolment queue

Citizens who apply for an eID online from home appear in the Enrolment Queue. Your job is to verify their information against the Civil Registry, review their documents and live photo, and make a decision.

Opening the queue

  1. Click Enrolment Queue in the left menu.
  2. Click Refresh queue to load the latest applications.

Filter tabs — what each tab shows

TabShows applications that are…
AllEvery application in the system
Pending reviewNew applications waiting for a first decision
Supervisor queueEscalated cases, cases awaiting documents, and cases where documents were re-submitted
EscalatedCases you or a colleague sent to a supervisor
Awaiting docsCases where you asked the citizen to upload more documents — waiting for them to respond
Docs receivedCitizen has re-uploaded documents — ready for supervisor review and approval

Understanding a queue card

Each application card shows:

  • Applicant name and a status badge
  • National ID (NID) and Session reference number
  • Submitted date and applicant email
  • Supervisor note (if any previous action was taken)

Status badges

BadgeMeaningYour next action
Pending review New application Click Process to review and decide
Escalated Sent to supervisor Click Supervisor review
Awaiting documents Waiting for citizen to re-upload Click Track — citizen must resume at the public enrolment page
Documents received Citizen has uploaded new documents Click Supervisor review then approve or reject

View vs Process

  • View — read-only look at the application (no decisions)
  • Process / Supervisor review / Track — opens the decision screen
When an application is Awaiting documents, tell the citizen to go to the public eID enrolment page and enter their session reference to upload the requested documents.

6. Process an application

When you click Process on a queue card, the Process eID Application screen opens. This is where you compare the citizen's submission with Civil Registry records and choose an outcome.

Step-by-step: reviewing an application

  1. Check the session reference at the top — this is the unique ID for this application.
  2. Load Civil Registry data. The system usually loads this automatically. If not, click Refresh registry.
    • Green banner: applicant details match the registry.
    • Yellow banner: mismatches found — review each field carefully.
    • Red banner: registry record not found — verify National ID and date of birth.
  3. Compare two columns:
    • Applicant submitted — what the citizen entered (you can correct typos here)
    • Civil Registry — official government record (read-only)
  4. Review documents and photos in the Submitted photos & documents section:
    • Live photo — portrait captured during online enrolment
    • ID front and ID back — photos of identity documents
    Click Download on any document to view it full size.
  5. Choose your decision (see table below).
  6. Select the SOP reason code — the system suggests the correct code based on your decision. Change it only if another code better describes the situation.
  7. Add verification notes (recommended for approvals with mismatches, rejections, and escalations).
  8. Click Confirm Approval, Confirm Rejection, or the matching confirm button for your decision.

Decision options

DecisionWhen to use itResult
Approve & Issue eID Identity verified; documents acceptable; registry matches (or mismatch explained in notes with SOP 01) Citizen receives eID number and UIN; account becomes active
Request Additional Documents Documents are missing, unclear, or poor quality Citizen must re-upload via the public enrolment page; status becomes Awaiting documents
Escalate to Supervisor Complex case needing senior review; unsure about approval Case moves to supervisor queue; no eID issued yet
Send to Fraud Review Suspected fraud, stolen identity, or serious data mismatch Case goes to Fraud Review page; no eID issued until supervisor resolves
Reject Application Applicant is ineligible or provided false information Application closed; citizen notified; no eID issued
You cannot approve an application while its status is Awaiting documents. Wait until the citizen re-uploads and the status changes to Documents received.

Approving when data does not match

If the applicant's name, date of birth, gender, nationality, or address does not match the Civil Registry:

  1. Read the yellow mismatch banner — it tells you how many fields differ.
  2. Correct obvious typos in the applicant column if the citizen made a data entry error.
  3. If the mismatch is genuine (for example, name spelling difference), do not approve without supervisor awareness.
  4. The SOP code automatically switches to 01 - Manual identity override.
  5. Write clear verification notes explaining why you approved despite the mismatch.
  6. Click Confirm Approval.

Rejecting an application

  1. Select Reject Application.
  2. Choose SOP code 04 - Applicant ineligible or 05 - Document quality insufficient.
  3. Add notes explaining the reason (these may be reviewed later).
  4. Click Confirm Rejection.

7. Supervisor workflow & document requests

Requesting additional documents from a citizen

  1. Open the application and select Request Additional Documents.
  2. Choose SOP code 03 - Request additional documents or 05 - Document quality insufficient.
  3. Write clear notes explaining exactly what the citizen must provide (for example, "Please upload a clearer photo of your ID front").
  4. Click Confirm Document Request.
  5. Tell the citizen to visit the public eID enrolment page and enter their session reference to upload the new documents.
  6. The application moves to the Awaiting docs tab. Use Track to monitor progress.

After the citizen re-uploads documents

  1. Go to the Docs received tab (or Supervisor queue).
  2. Click Supervisor review on the application.
  3. Review the newly uploaded documents and live photo.
  4. Approve with Confirm Approval or reject if still insufficient.

Escalating to a supervisor

  1. Select Escalate to Supervisor.
  2. Choose SOP 02 - Escalate to supervisor or 01 - Manual override required.
  3. Explain the situation in verification notes.
  4. Click Confirm Escalation.
  5. A supervisor picks up the case from the Escalated or Supervisor queue tab.

Sending to fraud review

  1. Select Send to Fraud Review.
  2. SOP code 99 - Suspected fraud / data mismatch is applied automatically.
  3. Add detailed notes about your suspicion.
  4. Click Confirm Fraud Review.
  5. Resolve the case on the Fraud Review page (Section 9).

8. In-person enrolment (counter service)

Use New Enrolment when a citizen comes to the PSC counter in person and does not have an active online application. This 7-step wizard verifies their identity, captures a live photo, and issues an eID immediately upon approval.

Before starting, ask the citizen for their National ID card, passport (if applicable), and proof of address if needed. Have the webcam ready for the live photo step.

The 7 steps

Step 1 — Personal details

Enter information exactly as it appears in the Civil Registry:

  • First name, last name, gender, nationality, date of birth
  • If the applicant is under 12 years old, also enter the parent or legal guardian name

Click Continue →

Step 2 — Contact information

  • Residential address
  • Email address (must be valid format)
  • Mobile number (include country code, for example +1 721 …)

Click Continue →

Step 3 — Identity documents

  • National ID (NID) — required; enter exactly as printed on the physical card
  • Passport number and expiry — optional unless verifying via ePassport

Click Continue →

Step 4 — ePassport verification

Choose one of two methods:

MethodWhen to useWhat to do
ePassport chip / MRZ Citizen has a biometric passport with a readable chip Click Read ePassport chip (NFC) or paste the MRZ lines, then click Verify MRZ against entered data. All fields should show green (match).
Documentary verification (Pattern B) No chip reader available, or document has no chip Select Pattern B and visually inspect the physical passport or ID. You take responsibility for manual document checks.

Click Continue →

Step 5 — Civil Registry verification

  1. Click Load Civil Registry.
  2. Compare Applicant entered vs Civil Registry side by side.
  3. Each field shows a Match or Mismatch badge.
  4. If mismatches exist, verify with the citizen before continuing.

Click Continue →

Step 6 — Live photo capture

  1. The camera starts automatically. Ask the citizen to face the camera directly.
  2. Click Capture photo when ready.
  3. If the camera is unavailable, click Upload photo instead (JPG, PNG, or WebP, max 5 MB).
  4. Use Retake photo if the image is unclear.

Click Continue →

Step 7 — Review & issue eID

  1. Review the full summary including the portrait preview.
  2. Select the SOP reason code:
    • 00 — Standard verification passed — everything matched cleanly
    • 01 — Manual override (supervisor) — you overrode a mismatch or used Pattern B
    • 99 — Rejected: suspected fraud — do not issue; stops the process
  3. Add verification notes if using code 01 or 99.
  4. Click Issue eID.

Success screen

When enrolment succeeds, you see:

  • The new eID number (for example, ID-SXM-26-…)
  • The citizen's UIN (Unique Identification Number)
  • Full name and National ID

Give the citizen their eID number. They can then sign in to the citizen portal to set up their password and MFA.

Click Enrol another citizen to start again, or View enrolment queue to return to online applications.

If the Civil Registry is offline, enrolment still works using cached records, but the UIN write-back is queued. Check the Reconciliation page after connectivity returns.

9. Fraud review

Cases sent for fraud review appear on the Fraud Review page. No eID is issued until a supervisor approves the case here.

Opening the fraud queue

  1. Click Fraud Review in the left menu.
  2. Use filter Open only to see active cases, or All statuses for history.
  3. Click Review on a case to open details.

Reviewing a fraud case

The detail panel shows:

  • Applicant personal details and National ID
  • Source of the case (online application or counter enrolment)
  • SOP reason code that was applied
  • Submitted documents: live photo, ID front, ID back

Supervisor decision

DecisionSOP codesOutcome
Approve & issue eID 00 - Standard verification passed
01 - Manual identity override		 eID and UIN issued; case closed as Approved
Reject — close case 04 - Applicant ineligible
99 - Confirmed fraud		 No eID issued; case closed as Rejected
  1. Review all evidence carefully.
  2. Select your decision and SOP code.
  3. Add verification notes explaining your reasoning.
  4. Click Confirm approval or Confirm rejection.
Do not approve a fraud case with SOP code 99 (confirmed fraud). Use rejection instead.

10. UIN write-back reconciliation

When the Civil Registry connection is offline, approved enrolments still create eID numbers locally, but the official UIN update to the Civil Registry is queued until connectivity returns. The Reconciliation page lets you replay these queued updates.

When to use this page

  • The dashboard shows Civil Registry offline mode is active
  • The dashboard stat shows write-backs awaiting replay
  • Your IT team confirms the registry connection is restored

Running reconciliation

  1. Click Reconciliation in the left menu.
  2. Review the summary counts: awaiting write-back, manual intervention, completed.
  3. Click Run reconciliation to process all queued items.
  4. Wait for the success summary showing processed, succeeded, and failed counts.
  5. For individual failed items, click Retry on that row.

Queue status meanings

StatusMeaningAction
PendingWaiting to be sent to Civil RegistryRun reconciliation
Retry scheduledFailed once; will retry automaticallyWait or click Retry
FailedCould not write backClick Retry; contact IT if it keeps failing
Manual interventionNeeds IT or registry office attentionContact your system administrator
CompletedSuccessfully written to Civil RegistryNo action needed

11. Audit logs

Every action in the eID system is recorded in the audit trail. Use this page to look up what happened for a specific citizen, session, or operator.

Searching audit records

  1. Click Audit Logs in the left menu.
  2. Use the search box: type an actor name, session reference, National ID, or action name.
  3. Click Search.

Quick filters

  • Outcome: All · Success · Failed · MFA pending · Blocked
  • Period: All time · Today · Last 7 days · Last 30 days
  • Stat chips at the top — click Total events, Today, Successful, or Failed / blocked

Advanced filters

For precise searches, expand advanced filters and fill in:

  • Subject ID (citizen UIN or NID)
  • Actor (operator who performed the action)
  • Action type (for example, PROCESS_ENROLMENT, ASSISTED_ENROLMENT)
  • Outcome and date range

Click Apply advanced filters or Reset everything to clear.

Reading a log entry

Each row shows: When · Action · Actor · Subject · Outcome · Source

Click More to expand full details including Event ID, category, IP address, and additional data.

Use Previous and Next at the bottom to browse pages.

Passwords and OTP codes are never shown in audit details. If you need a record of a specific enrolment decision, search by session reference or National ID.

12. Profile & security settings

Viewing your profile

  1. Click Profile in the left menu.
  2. View your username, eID number, email, mobile, role, and MFA status.
  3. Click View full account details for a complete read-only summary.
Profile fields (email, mobile, role) are read-only. Contact your system administrator to update account details.

Changing your password

  1. On the Profile page, find the Change password section.
  2. Enter your current password, new password, and confirm the new password.
  3. New password must be at least 8 characters.
  4. Click Update password.

Setting up an authenticator app (recommended)

  1. Click Security & MFA in the left menu.
  2. Under TOTP authenticator, click Set up authenticator app.
  3. Scan the QR code with Google Authenticator, Microsoft Authenticator, or similar.
  4. Enter the 6-digit code from the app to confirm.
  5. Your dashboard will now show account security as Protected.

Registering a passkey (optional)

  1. On the Security & MFA page, click Register passkey.
  2. Follow your browser's prompt (fingerprint, face, or device PIN).
  3. Next time you sign in, you can use the passkey instead of a password.

Disabling the authenticator app

  1. On Security & MFA, find the TOTP section.
  2. Enter your current authenticator code to confirm disable.
Disabling MFA reduces account security. Your administrator may require MFA for all PSC staff.

13. Troubleshooting common messages

Message you seeWhat it meansWhat to do
No Civil Registry record found NID or date of birth does not match any registry entry Ask the citizen to verify their NID and DOB. Contact the Civil Registry office if the citizen insists the details are correct.
Civil Registry live API is offline Live registry connection is down; cached records used Continue with caution using cached data. Run reconciliation when back online.
Processing failed Your decision could not be saved Try again. If it persists, note the session reference and contact IT support.
Camera permission denied Browser blocked camera access Allow camera in browser settings, or use Upload photo instead.
No camera found No webcam detected Connect a webcam or upload a photo file.
Camera requires a secure connection Page not served over HTTPS Use the official government URL (HTTPS). Contact IT if the problem continues.
Enrolment failed Counter enrolment could not complete Check all required fields and Civil Registry load. Try again or escalate.
Could not load fraud review queue Temporary system error Click Refresh. Contact IT if the page stays empty.
Reconciliation failed Batch replay did not complete Check Civil Registry is online. Retry individual items.
Invalid code. Try again. Wrong authenticator or OTP code Wait for a new code (TOTP rotates every 30 seconds) and re-enter carefully.
Loading PSC portal… (stuck) Session expired or network issue Refresh the page. Sign in again if redirected to login.

When to contact your system administrator

  • You cannot sign in after multiple attempts
  • Your role or email needs updating
  • Reconciliation items stay in Manual intervention
  • Civil Registry offline for an extended period
  • You suspect unauthorized access to your account
