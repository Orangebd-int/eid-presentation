1. Introduction

Your eID (electronic identity) is your secure digital identity for Sint Maarten. Once enrolled, you can sign in to government and partner services with multi-factor authentication, and control what personal information you share with each service.

This guide covers your full journey as a citizen:

  • Apply online from home using the enrolment form
  • Apply in person at a Public Service Center (PSC) counter
  • Resume an incomplete application or re-upload documents when PSC asks
  • Sign in with password, SMS, authenticator app, or passkey
  • Recover access if you lose your phone or authenticator
  • Use government portals (tax, business registration, etc.) through secure federation login
PSC operators review every application against the Civil Registry before an eID is issued. Processing times vary — keep your application reference safe until you receive confirmation.

2. Before you apply

Who can apply online?

  • Residents aged 12 and older with a valid National ID (NID) registered in the Civil Registry
  • Applicants under 12 must enrol in person at a PSC office with a parent or legal guardian

What you need for online enrolment

ItemDetails
DeviceComputer, tablet, or smartphone with a modern browser (Chrome, Safari, Edge)
CameraFor a live photo of your face (or upload a photo if camera is unavailable)
National IDYour NID number and physical ID card to photograph (front and back)
Contact detailsValid email address and mobile number (used for status updates and MFA)
Civil Registry matchName and date of birth should match official registry records

What you need for in-person enrolment

  • Valid physical identity documents (passport or national ID)
  • Parent or guardian present if the applicant is a minor
  • Visit a PSC office during operating hours — no online form required
Have your documents ready before starting. You can save progress and return later using your application reference if you need to pause.

4. Online self-enrolment

Start here: Public homepage → Apply for eID · URL: /enrol

The enrolment wizard has 5 steps. Use Continue to move forward and Back to revise earlier steps.

Step 1 — Personal details

  • First name and last name — exactly as on Civil Registry records
  • Gender and nationality / citizenship
  • Date of birth — must match registry; under-12 applicants are blocked from online enrolment

On Step 1 you can click Resume application if you already have a reference number (see Section 6).

Step 2 — Contact information

  • Residential address
  • Email address — used for notifications
  • Mobile number — include country code; used for SMS verification at login

Step 3 — Identity documents

  • National ID (NID) — your official identification number
  • Passport number and expiry (if applicable)

Step 4 — Live photo & documents

  • Live photo — capture your portrait using your device camera (look straight at the camera, good lighting)
  • ID front — photograph or upload the front of your identity document (JPEG, PNG, WebP, or PDF; max 5 MB)
  • ID back — upload if your document has a back side
Blurry, cropped, or unreadable documents may cause rejection or a request for re-submission. Ensure all text on your ID is clearly visible.

Step 5 — Review & consent

  1. Review all entered details on the summary screen. Click any section heading to jump back and edit.
  2. Read the privacy and consent terms.
  3. Check the consent box to confirm you agree.
  4. Click Submit application.
After submission you receive an application reference (format SXM-SESSION-…). Write it down or copy it — you need it to resume or re-submit documents.

5. After you submit

Success screen

When submission succeeds, a confirmation dialog shows:

  • Your application reference with a Copy button
  • A timeline of what happens next

What happens next (online applications)

  1. A PSC officer reviews your documents and details.
  2. Your information is checked against the Civil Registry.
  3. You are notified by email or SMS when a decision is made.
  4. If approved, you receive your eID number and can sign in with the credentials provided.
You do not receive an active eID immediately after online submission. Wait for PSC approval unless you enrolled in person at the counter.

6. Resume your application

If you closed the browser before finishing, or saved partway through, you can continue later using your application reference.

  1. Go to /enrol.
  2. On Step 1 (Personal details), click Resume application.
  3. Enter your application reference (for example, SXM-SESSION-MARIA01).
  4. Click confirm — your saved fields and uploaded documents are restored.
  5. Continue from the step where you left off, complete any missing items, and submit.
If the reference is not found, check for typos. References are case-sensitive. Contact the PSC if you lost your reference and cannot recover it.

7. Re-submit documents

Sometimes a PSC officer needs clearer photos or additional documents before they can approve your application. Your application status becomes Awaiting documents.

How you know documents are requested

  • Email or SMS notification from the eID platform (when configured)
  • Instructions from the PSC officer, including your application reference

How to re-upload

  1. Go to /enrol and click Resume application.
  2. Enter your application reference.
  3. A blue banner appears: Additional documents requested, with the officer's note.
  4. You are taken directly to Step 4 — Live photo & documents.
  5. Replace or add the requested files (live photo, ID front, ID back as needed).
  6. Click Submit application.
  7. Enter the 6-digit verification code sent to your registered mobile number.
  8. Confirm submission — you see the success screen again.
Document re-submission requires SMS verification to protect your application from unauthorized changes. In demo/training environments the code may appear on screen instead of a real SMS.

After you re-submit

Your status changes to Documents received in the PSC queue. An officer will review the new files and approve or reject your application. You do not need to take further action unless contacted again.

8. In-person enrolment at PSC

If you cannot apply online, are under 12, or prefer face-to-face service, visit a Public Service Center (PSC) office.

What to expect

  1. Bring valid identity documents and (for minors) your parent or legal guardian.
  2. Tell the counter officer you need to enrol for an eID.
  3. The operator verifies your identity against the Civil Registry, may scan your ePassport chip, and captures a live photo.
  4. If verification passes, your eID number and UIN are issued during the visit.
  5. You receive your login details and set up MFA on first sign-in.

For operator procedures, see the PSC Operator User Manual (staff use only).

9. Application outcomes

After PSC review, your online application receives one of these outcomes:

OutcomeWhat it means for youWhat to do
Approved Your eID is issued; account is active Sign in with your eID number or email and the password provided. Set up MFA.
Awaiting documents Officer needs better or additional files Re-submit via /enrol with your reference (Section 7)
Under review Application in the PSC queue Wait for notification; do not submit duplicate applications
Rejected Application declined (ineligible, fraud concern, poor document quality) Contact PSC for explanation; you may need to re-apply in person with corrected documents

Escalation and fraud review

Some applications are escalated to a supervisor or fraud review team. This does not mean you did something wrong — it is a standard control when data mismatches or identity concerns arise. Processing may take longer. You will be contacted if more information is needed.

10. Sign in to your eID

URL: /login

Standard sign-in (password)

  1. Click Sign in on the homepage.
  2. Enter your eID number (format ID-SXM-…) or email address.
  3. Enter your password and click continue.
  4. Complete the second verification step (see below).
  5. You arrive at your Citizen Dashboard at /citizen/dashboard.

Second verification (MFA)

Every sign-in requires a second step after your password:

MethodHow it works
SMS code6-digit code sent to your registered mobile number
Authenticator app6-digit code from Google Authenticator, Microsoft Authenticator, or similar (if you enabled TOTP)

If both SMS and authenticator app are available, choose the tab for your preferred method on the MFA screen.

Sign in with passkey (optional)

  1. On the login page, switch to the Passkey tab.
  2. Enter your eID number.
  3. Follow your device prompt (fingerprint, Face ID, Windows Hello, or security key).
Passkeys must be registered first under Security settings (Section 12).

Sign out

Use Sign Out from the citizen navigation menu when you finish your session, especially on shared computers.

11. Dashboard & profile

Citizen dashboard

URL: /citizen/dashboard (after sign-in)

  • Welcome message with your name, eID number, and account status
  • Identity summary — National ID, date of birth
  • Contact summary — email and masked mobile number
  • Security summary — MFA method and passkey status
  • Quick links to Profile and Security settings

Profile page

URL: /citizen/profile

  • View your UIN, eID number, National ID, email, mobile, and account status
  • Core identity fields are read-only — contact PSC to correct registry data
  • Change password (current password + new password required)
  • Update email or mobile with OTP confirmation
After MFA recovery (Section 14), a banner on your profile reminds you to set up new MFA before your next login.

12. Security settings

URL: /citizen/security

SMS verification

Always active on your registered mobile — used at every sign-in unless you use a passkey.

Authenticator app (TOTP) — recommended

  1. Click Set up authenticator app.
  2. Scan the QR code with your authenticator app.
  3. Enter the 6-digit code to confirm.
  4. At next login you can choose SMS or authenticator app.

Passkey (optional)

  1. Click Register passkey.
  2. Complete your browser or device security prompt.
  3. Use passkey sign-in on supported devices for faster, passwordless access.
Enable an authenticator app even if you rely on SMS. It protects you if you change phone numbers or travel abroad.

13. Government services login

Many government portals (for example, tax services or business registration) use your eID to sign in securely. You do not create a separate password on each site.

How federation login works

  1. Open a government service website (for example, Tax Portal or COCI).
  2. Click Sign in with eID or similar.
  3. You are redirected to the official eID login page.
  4. Sign in with your eID credentials and complete MFA.
  5. You are returned to the service — now logged in.
Privacy: Each service receives only the identity information it is authorised to see. You may be asked to consent before data is shared. The eID platform uses privacy-preserving tokens where configured.

Developer sandbox (optional)

Integrators test OIDC connections at /sandbox. Citizens normally use real government portals, not the sandbox.

14. Lost access recovery

Use this if you forgot your MFA method, lost your phone, or cannot receive SMS codes — but you still know your password and identity details.

URL: /recover (linked from the login page as Lost access?)

Step 1 — Verify identity

  1. Enter your eID number or email and password.
  2. Enter your date of birth exactly as registered.
  3. Enter your mother's maiden name as recorded in the Civil Registry.
  4. Click Verify identity.

Step 2 — Confirm with SMS

  1. Enter the 6-digit code sent to your registered mobile.
  2. Click Confirm recovery.
  3. Your old authenticator app and passkey factors are reset.
  4. You are signed in and redirected to your profile.

After recovery

  1. Go to Security settings immediately.
  2. Set up a new authenticator app and/or passkey.
  3. Verify you can sign in again with the new MFA setup.
If identity verification fails (for example, wrong mother's maiden name), you cannot recover online. Visit your nearest PSC office with valid ID for assisted recovery.

15. Special cases

Minors (under 12)

Online enrolment is blocked with the message that applicants under 12 must enrol in person at a PSC office with a parent or legal guardian present.

Deceased persons

Accounts marked deceased in the Civil Registry cannot sign in. Login is blocked with an appropriate message. Contact the registry office if you believe this is an error.

Wrong password lockout

After several failed password attempts, your account is temporarily locked for security. Wait for the lockout period to expire, or contact support if you forgot your password entirely.

Name or registry mismatches

If your online application name does not exactly match the Civil Registry (for example, spelling of a family name), PSC may request documents, escalate to a supervisor, or reject the application. Ensure your enrolment details match official records, or visit PSC in person.

When to visit PSC in person

  • You lost your application reference and need to track an application
  • Online recovery failed identity verification
  • Your application was rejected and you need to understand why
  • You are enrolling a child under 12
  • Registry data (name, DOB, NID) needs correction before enrolment can succeed
