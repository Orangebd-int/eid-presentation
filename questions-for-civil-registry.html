Government of Sint Maarten · eID Programme

Civil Registry alignment meeting

We start with eID, then UIN. The eID Platform does not store Civil Registry person data — verify and profile are live lookups only. We also need to agree how suspend, deactivate, and revoke work for both eID and UIN.

For CEO & Civil Registry eID first · UIN second August 2026

eID

Age 12+: verify, enrol, live profile. Login can be suspended, deactivated, or revoked.

UIN

Issued at birth (BRN-style). Under 12: UIN only. UIN can be suspended or revoked.

Rule

No Civil Registry record is copied into the eID Platform.

Part A — eID Platform

1 Who can get an eID

  1. Confirm: under 12 = no eID. Cut-off: on the 12th birthday, or the day after?
  2. Is Civil Registry the only authority that can say “this person may apply for eID”?
  3. If the person already has a UIN from birth, do they keep that same UIN on the eID?

2 Verify before eID enrolment

We call Civil Registry once, check the person, then continue enrolment. We will not keep your payload.

  1. What do we send (national ID, name, date of birth, UIN if they have one, …)?
  2. What do you return so we can proceed (match yes/no, status, reason)? Which fields are mandatory?
  3. On failure (no match, deceased, under 12, mismatch): which reason codes should the citizen see?
  4. Does this verify call also confirm the UIN exists and is active for that person, or is that a separate check?

3 Citizen profile — live from Civil Registry, not stored

After enrolment, the citizen opens “my data” on the eID Platform. We cannot store Civil Registry records. We only show what you return at that moment.

  1. Will Civil Registry provide a live lookup API (by national ID and/or UIN) every time the citizen opens their profile?
  2. Which fields may we display? Which must never leave Civil Registry?
  3. May we keep the response only in the browser session / a few seconds in memory, or must every screen refresh call you again?
  4. If Civil Registry is unavailable, the profile cannot show CR data. Is that accepted, and what message should we show?
  5. When data changes at Civil Registry, the next lookup shows the new values. Confirm: no CR copy is stored on the eID Platform.

4 eID — suspend, deactivate, revoke

This is the eID login / account, not the UIN itself. We need Civil Registry’s role when a person dies, is flagged, or must lose access.

  1. Which events require suspend (temporary, can restore), deactivate (closed, may re-enrol later), or revoke (permanent, no restore)?
  2. For death: does Civil Registry notify us, and how soon must eID login stop?
  3. Who may request eID suspend/deactivate/revoke: Civil Registry only, eID admin, court/police, or the citizen?
  4. If Civil Registry sends a status (deceased, invalid, blocked), do we treat that as an automatic eID stop, or do we wait for a separate instruction?
  5. After suspend: who may reactivate the eID, and must Civil Registry confirm again?
Part B — UIN (at birth and later)

5 UIN at birth — under 12

  1. Confirm: at birth registration, Civil Registry requests a UIN the same day (BRN-style).
  2. What is the minimum data needed to issue that UIN?
  3. Under 12: UIN only — no eID, no login. Agreed?

6 UIN generator — Civil Registry calls us

Single request or bulk. We issue the UIN and return it. We store the UIN; we do not store Civil Registry person data.

  1. Required fields for single and bulk requests?
  2. Same person sent twice: return existing UIN or reject?
  3. Bulk: do you need per-row success/error?

7 UIN when someone later applies for eID

The person may already have a UIN from birth. We need one owner of “this UIN is valid”.

  1. At eID enrolment, does Civil Registry’s verify response confirm the UIN — or must we also call our own UIN API?
  2. If Civil Registry has no UIN but we issued one (or the reverse), what is the process before eID is allowed?
  3. Can someone enrol for eID without a birth UIN, or must a UIN exist first?

8 UIN — suspend, deactivate, revoke

UIN is the lifelong number. Stopping a UIN is separate from stopping an eID login — unless you tell us they must always move together.

  1. Which events need UIN suspend, deactivate, or revoke (death, fraud, duplicate, emigration, court order)?
  2. How does Civil Registry tell us (API call, file, event)? How quickly after death must the UIN stop being usable?
  3. If a UIN is revoked or suspended, must the matching eID automatically stop the same day?
  4. If someone has a UIN but no eID yet (child under 12), does suspend/revoke still apply to the UIN alone?
  5. Can a revoked UIN ever be reissued or reused, or is that number retired forever?

✓ Decisions before we leave

  • eID: under 12 = no eID; verify request/response; live profile, no CR copy stored.
  • eID stop: suspend vs deactivate vs revoke; who initiates; death timing; reactivation.
  • UIN: same-day at birth; generator fields; who confirms UIN at eID enrolment.
  • UIN stop: events, how you notify us, whether eID stops automatically, reuse of numbers.
